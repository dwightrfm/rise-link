# RISE Link: sender follow-up playbook
The page books the chat. These texts make sure it happens.

## When the prospect texts you after booking (reply within 5 minutes)
"Got it, [name], you're set. I told [host] your main thing is [what they picked in Q3], so he'll start there. Talk soon."

## The day before (send it yourself, from your phone)
"Quick heads-up: [host] has your time held tomorrow at [time], and he's prepped around [their Q3 pick]. Still good?"

## The morning of
"Today at [time]. The Zoom link is in your email invite. See you there."

## If they no-show (about 2 hours after)
"Missed you today. No stress at all. Want me to grab another time this week?"

Rules: soonest bookings show up most. If someone books more than a week out, offer a sooner time in your first reply. A reply that cancels is a win over a silent no-show: reschedule it on the spot. Judge this funnel on shows per 100 links sent, not bookings.
